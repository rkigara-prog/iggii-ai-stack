## What was fixed

The original inline expression failed when the prompt contained nested closing braces. PR #18 encoded the prompt to get past that parser; its continuation also reconstructed topic groups by source-ID order because the exact packet was missing. That continuation did not demonstrate an uninterrupted pipeline.

The Code node now builds a normal request object, with prompts and evidence held as data. The HTTP node evaluates only `={{ $json.requestBody }}`. The focused offline check reproduced the original expression failure in the installed n8n evaluator and proved that the new construction preserves braces and quotes. This fixes the pipeline's underlying request-construction defect without a vendor-engine patch or service restart.

The extractor now selects Microsoft article prose instead of related cards and recognizes CISA's paragraph-free text containers. A generic canonical homepage cannot replace a document URL. Old successful cache entries are re-extracted from saved HTML into new immutable snapshots; original snapshots remain. The shortlist keeps actual topic groups and allocates its existing 18-URL budget across groups rather than exhausting it on the earliest groups.

Qualification normalizes whitespace, Unicode and typographic quotes without substituting words. An exact official quotation explicitly requesting the existing exception can acquire its required publisher attribution; paraphrases cannot. The reviewed authority registry now includes the Canadian Cyber Centre, National Laboratory of the Rockies and Southern Company for their own document statements. News reporting does not become primary evidence, and unknown or shared origins do not become independent corroboration.
