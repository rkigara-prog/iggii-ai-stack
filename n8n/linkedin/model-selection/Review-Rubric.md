# Review rubric

This rubric is fixed before reading candidate comparison drafts. Scores below are
**Codex judgments**, not deterministic verification, independent human ratings or
measured editing effort. Keep dimensions separate; do not hide factual defects in
a weighted fluency average.

| Dimension | 0–1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factual support | Material fabrication/contradiction or pervasive unsupported claims | Multiple substantive unsupported assertions | One material assertion needs correction or qualification | Supported overall; minor scope/precision issue | Every material factual assertion is supported by the supplied passages with scope preserved |
| Attribution | Invented experience/telemetry or misleading ownership | Repeated ambiguity about whose evidence is described | One material attribution ambiguity | Attribution clear with a minor omission | Publisher, observation/publication dates and limits are clear throughout |
| Citation accuracy | Fabricated or wrong sources | Multiple incorrect references or unsupported bindings | One material binding problem | References correct; minor mapping omission | Correct URLs and every material assertion traceable to appropriate supplied passages |
| Useful insight | No useful editorial argument | Mostly repeats facts or generic advice | Relevant decision, limited development | Clear decision and useful bounded implications | Coherent, specific argument that improves a reader's decision without inventing evidence |
| Specificity | Generic output transferable to any topic | Few concrete details | Some useful topic-specific detail | Concrete audience decisions tied to the evidence | Precise distinctions and implications throughout, without filler or overreach |
| Readability | Confusing or unusable | Disjointed, bloated or major rewriting needed | Understandable with awkward sections | Clear connected prose with minor friction | Engaging opening, coherent progression, natural concise language |
| Low generic filler | Mostly boilerplate | Considerable slogans/checklist padding | Some unnecessary generic phrasing | Little unnecessary material | Every paragraph advances this specific argument |

For each draft, review complete prose sentence by sentence. Record an evidence ID
for each factual assertion, or flag unsupported/inference/advice. A missing assertion
in the writer's claim list still counts. Preserve the model's text; do not silently
edit it before blind review. Clearly framed recommendations need not appear as
source facts, but factual premises within them still need support.

A source excerpt that matches literally is only a mechanical pass. A Codex decision
that it supports a claim is semantic judgment. Claim-support gold and ranking
priorities inherited from the previous evaluation are also Codex judgments.

Report serious factual failures even when a draft reads well. Do not estimate
human editing minutes from these scores. Robert and Leigh record actual time only
when they edit. The independent score sheet deliberately leaves those fields blank.
